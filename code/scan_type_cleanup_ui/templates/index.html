Meningioma Project: Scan Type Cleanup App

Version 1.0.0

Welcome to the Meningioma Project's Scan Type Cleanup App!

Instructions

The app aims to provide a simple GUI to facilitate the hand-labelling of MRI scans in the Meningioma dataset. Once signing in, you will be presented with a series of MRI scans, including the middle slice of the scan's axial plane (when available), and accompanying metadata about the scan. You will then be asked to select the appropriate scan type for the given MRI from a drop-down menu. A note on special options:

  • The 'OTHER' option offers a text box to type the proper scan type in by hand, should the drop-down menu lack an appropriate option. Please only use this sparingly, and try to be as consistent as possible in your naming schema.
  • The 'DISCARD' option indicates the MRI scan is not useful for the project, and therefore does not require preprocessing.

You can take a break and save your progress at any point by clicking on the 'Save & Exit' button. Your progress will be saved on Calliope. To resume where you left off, simply sign back into the app using the same (case-sensitive) Username as before.

Your completed work will be stored as a CSV file on Lawrence Chillrud's Calliope account.

Important notes

  • As of v.1.0.0, only one person can sign in and work on the Scan Type Cleanup App at a time.
  • Please enter your FirstNameLastName as your Username when signing in, to ensure multiple users do not inadvertently adopt the same Username; as of v.1.0.0, multiple people assuming the same Username breaks the backend recording of submissions.
  • You must click the 'Save & Exit' button or finish labelling all scans before exiting the application webpage.
  • If you have any bugs, feature requests, or questions, please open an issue here.
